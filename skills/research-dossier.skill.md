Turn a subject into a dossier the user can trust — researched wide, checked
across sources, composed once.

## Procedure

1. **Scope** — break the subject into three to five concrete questions. Write
   them down first (they become the dossier's sections). A vague subject gets
   sharpened, not padded: "research the rand" → its level and trend, the
   drivers this month, the risks ahead, what the banks are forecasting.
2. **Search** — one search_web per question, phrased as that question, plus
   get_news on the subject for anything breaking. Follow up with a second
   search where the first answer is thin or old.
3. **Cross-check** — any fact that would change a decision (a number, a date,
   a claim about a person or company) must appear in at least two independent
   sources. Where sources disagree, the dossier says so and shows both.
4. **Compose** — the dossier:
   - a five-line executive summary a busy person could act on;
   - one section per scoped question, tight paragraphs, numbers dated;
   - a "what would change this picture" section — the two or three events
     that would invalidate the summary;
   - sources, named.
5. Offer the dossier for the HUD; keep the spoken version to the executive
   summary alone.

## Discipline

- Dated facts carry their date. "Recently" is banned.
- No source, no claim. A striking fact that appears nowhere else is flagged
  as single-source, not laundered into certainty.
- If the subject touches the user's money or health, the dossier reports —
  it never prescribes.
