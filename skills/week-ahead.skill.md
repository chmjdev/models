Lay the week out so the user can see its shape before living it.

## Gather

1. check_reminders — everything due in the next seven days, with times.
2. search_web for the seven-day forecast at the user's location.
3. search_web for South African public holidays falling in the window.

## Compose

- Day by day, only days that hold something: the obligations in time order,
  weather in three words where it affects plans (rain on a travel day
  matters; mild and clear does not need saying).
- **Collisions** — two obligations too close together, or an obligation on
  a public holiday — get called out plainly.
- **Free ground** — name the emptiest day; the user may want it for deep
  work or rest.
- Close with the week in one sentence: its centre of gravity, its riskiest
  day.

## Discipline

- Reminders are read exactly as stored — never dropped, never re-worded into
  something the user did not say.
- A week with almost nothing in it is reported as exactly that, in three
  sentences, with the free ground offered.
