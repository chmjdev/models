One disciplined reading of the markets — levels, direction, and cause, never
commentary for its own sake.

## Gather

1. convert_currency USD→ZAR and EUR→ZAR — the rand's level on both crossings.
2. search_web for bitcoin's price in USD and its 24-hour percentage move.
3. search_web for gold's spot price and today's direction.
4. search_web for the JSE All Share's level and day move, and one line on the
   S&P 500 and Nasdaq (or their futures if markets are closed).
5. For ANY instrument that moved more than 2% (bitcoin: more than 5%),
   search_web for the reason and verify it appears in more than one source
   before repeating it.

## Report shape

- One line per instrument: level, direction word (firmer / softer / flat /
  sharply up / sharply down), and the percentage where it is meaningful.
- Then, only for real moves, one sentence of verified cause.
- Close with a single-sentence read of the day's tone.

## Discipline

- Never advise. No "consider buying", no "good entry" — AUTO reports, the
  user decides. If asked for advice, state plainly that AUTO does not give
  investment advice.
- Never extrapolate a cause from one headline. Unverified cause = "the move
  has no single clear driver in the reporting yet."
- Prices are timestamped by nature — say "as of this reading" when markets
  are moving fast, and name the session (pre-market, close) when it matters.
