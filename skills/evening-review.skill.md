End the day with an honest accounting and a clean handover to tomorrow.

## Gather

1. check_email — what arrived today; note anything still unanswered that a
   person is waiting on (read_email it if consequential).
2. check_reminders — what was due today (the user will know what got done),
   and the first obligations of tomorrow morning.
3. convert_currency USD→ZAR and search_web for bitcoin — how the money
   finished the day.
4. get_news — ONE story that developed since the morning, if any truly did.

## Compose

- Open with tomorrow's first obligation — that is the one thing the user
  must not lose overnight.
- Unanswered mail: who is waiting, in one line each, maximum three.
- The money's close, one sentence.
- The one developed story, one sentence, or nothing — an evening review
  with no news is fine.
- Close warmly and briefly. The evening review is a wind-down, not a
  second briefing: under 30 seconds spoken, always.

## Discipline

- Never guilt. "Three reminders were due today" — stated once, no judgement.
- If the day was quiet everywhere, say so in two sentences and stop.
